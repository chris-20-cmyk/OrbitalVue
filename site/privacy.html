Privacy at StreamVue

A plain-language view of what the app stores, where it goes, and what StreamVue never receives.

Draft for owner review — not yet a published Store policy.

Summary

You choose the source. StreamVue stays out of the middle.

StreamVue is a media player. It does not provide channels or media, operate an advertising network, or require a developer-run account. You connect the app to IPTV playlists or personal Plex and Emby servers that you own or are authorized to access.

Automatic telemetry, advertising, cross-app tracking, and the sale of personal data are disabled in the reviewed app foundation.

Data on your device

Playback data is kept locally.

StreamVue stores the information needed to provide the player experience on your device. This can include a playlist address or imported file, server address, channel and guide metadata, Plex or Emby library metadata, favorites, recent items, resume state, and playback or interface preferences.

Media-center credentials use platform protection when available, with a session-only fallback. Playlist locations use app-private storage with protection that depends on the platform.

Connections you request

Requests go to the provider you configure.

When you connect or play something, StreamVue sends the required address, authentication information, access token, or user-supplied request headers to the IPTV, Plex, or Emby provider you selected. The StreamVue developer does not receive those provider credentials.

Your device→Your chosen provider

For unlocked personal media centers, the app may create a random installation identifier and share it with your selected Plex or Emby server so the server can recognize the player.

Purchases

Platform Stores verify premium access.

Store builds use the platform's purchase system. The Store product identifier and entitlement result may be stored on the device. Android and Samsung verifier code is designed to process only the minimum purchase-verification values without request-body logging or identifiers in its response.

The verifier is not deployed and premium verification remains release-locked today. Its production host, retention rules, Store disclosures, and real-device evidence must be reviewed before the public policy is approved or the service receives data.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics remain local unless you share them.

The Windows app can keep local crash details, runtime and operating-system information, catalog counts, hashed channel identity, playback state, and preferences for troubleshooting. An export is created only when you request it. The developer receives diagnostic information only if you choose to share that export.

Before sharing a report, review it and remove playlist URLs, credentials, tokens, or private media names.

Retention & deletion

You control locally stored app data.

App data remains on the device until you remove a source, clear the relevant data in the app where that control is available, reset the app through the operating system, or uninstall it. Platform Stores and providers you choose apply their own retention and deletion policies to data they receive.

Exact per-platform deletion instructions are still under owner and device review and must be completed before this draft becomes a published Store policy.

Contact

A verified privacy contact is still required.

This draft does not publish an unverified email address. A monitored privacy contact, effective date, and final policy owner will be added before any Store candidate can pass its release checks.

For current technical help, use the StreamVue support page. Do not post credentials, access tokens, private playlist addresses, or personal media details in a public issue.

At a glance
  • No advertising
  • No cross-app tracking
  • No StreamVue account
  • No automatic telemetry
  • No sale of personal data
